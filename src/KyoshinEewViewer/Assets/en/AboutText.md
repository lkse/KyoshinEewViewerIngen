## About the Predicted Seismic Intensity Distribution

The arrival time estimation circles are based on linear interpolation of the JMA2001 travel-time table.  
Due to the nature of the travel-time table, circles with a radius of 2000 km or more are not displayed.

## About Earthquake Information

### [JMA Disaster Prevention XML (PULL)](https://xml.kishou.go.jp/xmlpull.html)

Compared to tools that scrape data from other websites, this method is slower,  
but it is more reliable and less affected by specification changes.

### [Project DM-D.S.S](https://dmdata.jp/)

Although it is a paid service, it is currently the fastest way for individuals to receive telemeter data.
