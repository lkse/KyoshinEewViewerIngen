## What is [VOICEVOX](https://voicevox.hiroshiba.jp/)?

A free, high-quality text-to-speech and singing synthesis software.

In KyoshinEewViewer for ingen, trigger events from the workflow system can be announced by voice as an action.  
By default, VOICEVOX is available as an action when it is running on the same PC.

VOICEVOX only performs speech synthesis; audio playback is handled by KyoshinEewViewer for ingen.  
Please note that there may be a slight delay (a few seconds or more) between synthesis and actual audio playback.
